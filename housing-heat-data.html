Reference · Data for housing, heat & health research

Housing, heat & health data

A working repository of datasets spanning housing conditions, urban heat, and population health — anchored in Paris and France, with a closing section for work set elsewhere.

Most entries are point data or regular grids, not administrative boundaries — budget time for a join or interpolation step to get to the commune, IRIS, or neighbourhood level a project needs.

How to use this page
  • Filter by theme below, or scroll — every section stands on its own.
  • France/Paris entries are the deep core; the last section covers other geographies for work set elsewhere.
  • Found a dead link or a better source? Worth updating so this stays current.
